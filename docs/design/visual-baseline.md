# UBWENZI — Approved visual baseline

**Status:** The user-approved mockup is the binding visual reference for the public waitlist experience.

## Non-negotiable characteristics
- Warm blush, soft pink and peach backgrounds with gentle gradients.
- Deep plum / near-black typography and vivid fuchsia primary actions.
- Heart-inspired UBWENZI mark paired with a bold wordmark.
- Large editorial hero headline with highlighted words in fuchsia.
- Couple-led hero composition with a mobile-device product preview.
- Translucent rounded value strip for verified profiles, welcoming community and real relationships.
- Rounded interface surfaces, soft shadows and subtle borders.
- Mobile product previews showing onboarding, profile, discovery and messaging.
- Responsive adaptation without losing this visual identity.

## Directional palette
- Primary fuchsia: #DF0753 (approximation from the supplied mockup; refine when brand tokens are formally locked).
- Deep plum: #5B1238.
- Ink: #281225.
- Blush: #FFF1F0.
- Peach: #F8D6C7.
- Warm white: #FFF8F6.

## Market adaptation
English is the default public language for the Lagos launch; French remains supported in the product direction. Copy should communicate serious relationships, friendship and intentional discovery.

The primary pre-launch action is **Join the waitlist**, not member account creation. Do not present a fake login or claim that waitlist registration is active until API persistence, consent records, email verification and abuse controls exist and have passed tests.
